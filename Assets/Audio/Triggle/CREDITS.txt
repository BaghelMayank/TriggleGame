GENERATED AUDIO
---------------

The win fanfare is synthesised at runtime by SoundManager (a rising C-E-G-C
arpeggio with an added fifth). No suitable CC0 fanfare was available from the
sources above, so rather than ship a poor match it is generated in code. Drop a
real clip into SoundManager's "Win Fanfare Clip" field to replace it.
